# Conventions

## Commit messages

- Be concise and describe what was done, not that it was done.
- No filler like "we have created", "added the ability to", etc. — just state the change.
- Professional, direct tone.
- Prefix every message with its type: `feat:`, `fix:`, `bug:`, `chore:`, `docs:`, `refactor:`.

Example: `feat: rename recording buttons in interview section`
